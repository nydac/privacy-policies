Privacy Policy

Effective 4 October 2026 · applies to version 0.1.0 and later

At a glance

No personal data collected
We never see who you are, where you are, or what you do in the game.
No internet access
The app requests no Android permissions, not even network access.
No ads, analytics or tracking
No third-party advertising, analytics or social software is included.
No accounts or purchases
There is nothing to sign in to and nothing to buy.

1Who this policy covers

This policy describes how the mobile game Aethelgard Covenant (Android package com.aethelgard.covenant) handles information. "We" means the independent developer of the game.

2Information we collect

None. The game does not collect, store on any server, sell or share personal information. It has no sign-in, no online features, and requests no Android permissions, including internet access, so it is technically unable to send information off your device.

3Information kept on your device

To work properly the game saves a small amount of data in its own private storage on your phone. This data never leaves the device and contains nothing that identifies you:

Game settings
The graphics quality chosen for your hero, so the game starts at a level your phone can run smoothly.
Graphics cache
Prepared shader data that the game engine stores to load faster on later launches.

You can delete this data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the game.

4Google Play

When you install the game through Google Play, Google may collect information such as installation, crash and performance statistics under its own terms. This is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Google may show us anonymous, aggregated statistics (for example, how many devices installed the game or how often it crashed); these do not identify you, and we do not receive personal information from Google about players.

5Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

6Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act or the EU General Data Protection Regulation), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. We hold no personal data about players, so there is nothing for us to provide or delete. Data stored on your own device can be removed as described in section 3.

7Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that handle personal information, such as online accounts, trading or multiplayer, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's release notes on Google Play.

8Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact email shown on the Aethelgard Covenant page on Google Play.
